Leonida Missions – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 November 2026

Leonida Missions is an unofficial fan-made guide. It is not affiliated with, endorsed, sponsored, or approved by Rockstar Games or Take-Two Interactive. Grand Theft Auto and GTA are trademarks of Take-Two Interactive.

The short version

The app has no accounts and no server of its own. Your progress stays on your device (and in your own iCloud, if you turn on iCloud sync). The app shows ads, and the ad providers collect some data to do that. You can limit this with the choices described below.

What the app itself stores

  • Your progress and notes (completed missions, found collectibles, notes, custom trackers) are saved on your device. We cannot see them.
  • iCloud sync (optional). If you turn it on, your progress is copied to your private iCloud database, run by Apple under your Apple Account. We cannot see it.
  • Settings such as your console, spoiler level and theme are saved on your device.

Files the app downloads

To get new guides and ad settings without an app update, the app downloads a few public files from GitHub. Like any web request, this shows your IP address to GitHub. No information about you is sent with it.

Advertising

Ads are provided by Google AdMob, with Unity Ads as an additional ad source. To show ads, limit how often you see the same one, measure them and prevent fraud, these providers collect data through their software in the app:

DataUsed for
Device identifiers, including the advertising identifier (only if you allow tracking)Advertising, analytics, fraud prevention
Approximate location, derived from your IP addressShowing ads suited to your country or region
How you interact with ads and with the app (launches, taps, ad views)Advertising, analytics
Diagnostics (crash and performance data from the ad software)Keeping the ad software working

We do not receive this data in a form that identifies you. For details, see how Google uses information from apps that use its services, the Google Privacy Policy and the Unity Game Player and App User Privacy Policy.

Your choices

  • Consent. Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland), the app asks for your ad consent before any ad is loaded. You can change your choice at any time in the app: Settings → Privacy options.
  • Tracking. iOS asks whether the app may use your advertising identifier. If you say no, you still see ads, but they are not based on that identifier. You can change this in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • US state privacy rights. Where applicable, you can opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information for advertising through Settings → Privacy options.
  • Remove Ads. A one-time purchase turns off banners, full-screen ads and app-open ads. Purchases are handled by Apple; we never see your payment details.
  • Delete your data. Settings → Reset progress erases everything the app stored. Deleting the app removes it from the device.

Children

The app is a guide to a game for mature audiences. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
